Mapping a multi-agent system from one injected prompt

One injected prompt, 70 scored runs across 10 workflows. Tool names and agent names leak from every architecture tried; the wiring between agents leaks only from the two patterns whose own messages carry it — handoffs, through the transfer notes left in the context, and mixture of agents, through the replies that travel back up.

All runs on gemini-3.5-flash-lite through AutoGen Core. Every figure below is taken from the run logs, not from what the agents claimed. The per-run tables and log sessions are in the companion write-ups in the POC folder.
